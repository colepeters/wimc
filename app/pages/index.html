May all beings flourish.

The Winnipeg Insight Meditation Community (WIMC) is a peer led, non sectarian sangha (or community) of meditation practitioners.

We share a commitment to the study & practice of the dharma — the teachings of Buddhism. As a non sectarian sangha, we draw on a range of traditions as diverse as our membership. Through our efforts, we work to bring awareness, compassion, and wisdom into our lives and the lives of the people and communities around us.

We meet each week on Sunday mornings for meditation and group discussion. All are welcome to join. To learn more, read about our weekly sessions. We recommend that those new to our sangha read our orientation before attending their first session.

As a peer led sangha, our areas of study and discussion change each week. To stay up to date with our sessions and any special events, subscribe to our newsletter.
